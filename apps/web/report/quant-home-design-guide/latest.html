Quant Home Design Guide

Current approved rules for the Market Pulse quant-model home dashboard.

Overview

The home page is a compact stock decision dashboard, not a landing page and not an auto-trading product.

Core Positioning
Market Pulse shows how a 퀀트 모델 reviews daily market data and explains today's stocks and market status in plain language. 
퀀트 모델이 고른 오늘의 종목
매일 시장 데이터를 계산해 살펴볼 종목, 기다릴 종목, 조심할 종목을 쉽게 정리합니다.

Top Panel

The first card is a gray split panel: today's stocks on the left, market status on the right.

Left: Today's Stocks
Title is 19px / 600. Show four compact metrics: 오늘 살펴볼 종목, 조심 신호, 모델 검증 수익률, 오늘 리포트.
Right: Market Status
Show four compact cards: 지수, 수급, 체크, 분위기. Do not use vague copy like 숨 고르기 구간.

Right Rail

The right rail is a utility rail, not the old insight/status rail.

1. 뉴스
Title-only list. Scrolls inside the news card. More button goes to /news.
2. 광고 영역
Future ad slot with AD label. This replaces Today Report.
3. 관심 폴더
Folder list scrolls inside the card. Management happens in My Page.

Icon Rules
Use circular stock initial badges, simple favorite stars, compact info dots, and profile circle. Do not use odd temporary unicode icons.

Mobile

Use responsive layout, not a separate m. site.

Bottom Navigation
시장 | 모델 | 홈 | 서비스 | 마이
Home is centered. Today decisions and reports are reached from home. My contains profile, favorite folders, memos, alerts, saved reports, account, and admin entry if applicable.
